# What are Enterprise Use Cases?

Enterprise Use Cases are **real-world automation scenarios** used by organizations to manage thousands of servers and applications.

Instead of performing repetitive tasks manually, Ansible automates them using Playbooks.

# 3. Zero-Downtime Deployment

```text id="xg4pm2"

↓
```

---

# 4. Blue-Green Deployment

Maintain two identical environments:

- Blue (Current Production)
- Green (New Version)

Workflow

```text id="kq8g5w"
Blue Environment

↓

Deploy to Green

↓

Testing

↓

Switch Traffic

↓

Green becomes Production
```

Benefits

- Easy rollback
- Minimal downtime
- Safe production releases

# Enterprise Best Practices

- Store Playbooks in Git.
- Use Roles for reusable automation.
- Store secrets in Ansible Vault.
- Separate Dev, Test, UAT, and Production inventories.
- Test Playbooks before production deployment.
- Use Tags for selective execution.
- Make Playbooks idempotent.
- Integrate with CI/CD pipelines.
- Monitor automation jobs.
- Document all automation workflows.

# Interview Questions

### Why is Ansible widely used in enterprise environments?

Because it automates repetitive infrastructure and application management tasks, improving consistency, speed, and reliability.

---

### What is Zero-Downtime Deployment?

A deployment strategy where users continue to access the application while the new version is deployed.

---

### What is Blue-Green Deployment?

A deployment strategy using two identical environments. Traffic is switched to the new environment after successful testing.

---

### What is Rolling Deployment?

A deployment strategy where servers are updated one at a time to keep the application available during deployment.

---

### Why automate patch management?

To improve security, reduce vulnerabilities, and ensure all systems remain up to date.

---

### Why automate SSL certificate renewal?

To prevent service outages caused by expired certificates.

---

### Why perform health checks after deployment?

To verify that the application and infrastructure are working correctly.

---

### Why use Ansible for disaster recovery?

It automates infrastructure rebuilding and application deployment, reducing recovery time.
